# Methodology – MedEvidence-AI

## 1. Overview

MedEvidence-AI is based on a verification-first workflow for AI-assisted clinical research.

The objective is not to replace clinical reasoning with artificial intelligence, but to explore how AI can support the organization and synthesis of clinical information while maintaining traceability to reliable evidence.

---

## 2. Workflow

The methodology follows several stages:

```text
Clinical Case
      ↓
Case Structuring
      ↓
Clinical Question
      ↓
Differential Diagnosis
      ↓
Missing Information
      ↓
Evidence Search
      ↓
AI-Assisted Synthesis
      ↓
Claim Extraction
      ↓
Source Verification
      ↓
Correction / Refinement
      ↓
Final Evidence-Backed Response
